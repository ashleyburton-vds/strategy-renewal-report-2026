The Stage column is the task's actual Teamwork workflow stage. Its colour groups those stages into the five bands used by the tiles and the partner bars: Deliverables is not started; In Progress, QA Ready, Ready For Review, Needs Edits and Edits Ready For Review are with fulfilment; With Client/Rep and Edits With Client/Rep are awaiting client approval; Approved, Implemented, Post as Draft and the client-implementation stages are approved. No stage set means the task sits outside the standard workflow and needs a look in Teamwork.

CSM is the Teamwork project owner. Assigned to is whoever holds the task itself, usually the SEO specialist or a team queue.

Notes and holds are entered here, not in Teamwork, and are shared with everyone who can open this page.
